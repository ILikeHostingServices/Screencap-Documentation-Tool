Features

What you get

Everything you need to turn one recording into a guide someone else can follow.

A built-in recorder that notes every click

Each step gets a red ring where you clicked and a first-draft caption such as Click "Next". Recordings from Snipping Tool, OBS Studio, or any other recorder work too.

  • Optional narration becomes captions, transcribed offline
  • On Windows, the recorder window hides itself from the recording

Point readers at the right button

Draw arrows, numbered markers, and text labels. A red box shows what changed since the previous step, and a zoomed inset can show a small checkbox up close.

Sensitive text blurred for you

Passwords, license keys, tokens, and IP and email addresses are found with OCR and blurred. An unblurred copy is kept alongside in case you need the details. Shown here in the dark theme.

Finished documents, not just pictures

Export a single-file HTML guide, a Word document from your own template, a PDF, or a PowerPoint deck with one slide per step and narration in the speaker notes.

  • Alt text on every screenshot for screen readers
  • Your company logo on the title
